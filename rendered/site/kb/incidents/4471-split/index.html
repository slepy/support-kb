Fixing the parser leaves the booking-v3 form and the one-day shifts broken. Do not close a report against the parser fix until you know which problem it is.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap exactly: 7 April becomes 4 July. App, email and the booking-v2 form.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom as parser, a different fix: the hint, not the parser. Form reports from booking-v3 tenants only.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, or differs between two timezones (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). No swap. The desk had already noted these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but counted them under the parser.

How a report is placed

  1. Count each report once, by its id. The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  2. Look up the tenant (src/lib/data/tenants), not the reporter’s browser. Intake’s tag is a guess from the browser locale and disagrees with the tenant often (column “intake tagged otherwise”).
  3. A day-first tenant reporting through a booking-v3 form is hint. Any other channel or form is parser.
  4. Read the body. If the date did not swap but moved by a day, it is shift: add its id to oneDayMove in desk-notes.json.

Why it was one ticket

Triage merges on the subject line (decision 0001). These three problems share subjects like “Wrong day on the invite”, so the rule put them together. The subject tells you the symptom. Only the tenant, the form and the body tell you which problem it is.
